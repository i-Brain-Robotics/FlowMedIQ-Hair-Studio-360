Assumptions, research & calculation method

Thickness presets and density presets are independently entered scenarios. A percentage change and an absolute micron change are alternative methods; the app never adds both.

Research reference: Qu et al. (2024) measured 61.55 → 63.93 μm with 1565 nm laser at week 10 (+2.38 μm). Lee et al. studied 1550 nm laser in female pattern hair loss, reporting 58 → 75 μm after 5 months. Related wavelengths and treatment schedules are not interchangeable evidence.

Qu et al., 1565 nm study · Lee et al., 1550 nm study · Alma TED manufacturer data

Density × measured area gives hair count. Added hairs are divided by hairs per graft × survival × visible growth to estimate a graft equivalent. Remaining grafts are rounded up within each zone and never below zero. Diameter is reported independently; grafts and hairs are distinct units.

Reported shedding stabilization is qualitative context and is not used as a numerical graft reduction. A report of reduced shedding is not a guarantee of complete cessation within a fixed number of weeks.
